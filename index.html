About Farah

Putting
Ward 5
first.

My name is Farah Ahmed, and I am proud to be a candidate for Windsor City Council in Ward 5.

I have called Ward 5 home for the past 15 years, living at the same address and building strong roots in the community I care about.

I attended J.L. Forster Secondary School in Windsor and later graduated from St. Clair College as a Welding Technician. I am a welder by trade, and my career has taught me the value of hard work, responsibility, teamwork and solving problems.

I believe those same values belong in City Hall. Good leadership starts with listening. That means hearing residents' concerns, understanding their ideas and working together to find practical solutions.

My goal is simple: help make Ward 5 safer, cleaner, stronger and a better place to live, work and raise a family.

What I Will Work For

Priorities for Ward 5

Practical priorities focused on the everyday needs of residents, families and local businesses.

PRIORITY 01

Support Local Businesses

Local businesses create jobs, strengthen neighbourhoods and keep our community moving. I will support small businesses and work toward an environment where local entrepreneurs can open, grow and succeed in Windsor.

PRIORITY 02

Better Housing & Infrastructure

Residents deserve safe and affordable places to live, along with roads, sidewalks, parks and public spaces that are properly maintained. I will push for practical improvements that make everyday life better.

PRIORITY 03

Safer & Stronger Neighbourhoods

Our neighbourhoods should be places where people feel safe and proud to live. I will advocate for cleaner streets, better parks, safer public spaces and strong community connections.

PRIORITY 04

Every Voice Matters

Residents deserve a councillor who listens. I will make myself available to the community, listen to concerns and make sure Ward 5 has a strong and respected voice at City Hall.

PRIORITY 05

A Better Future for Ward 5

I believe Ward 5 can be stronger. Together, we can support a local economy, improve city services, build safer neighbourhoods and create a community we are proud to call home.

PRIORITY 06

Responsible Leadership

Residents deserve accountability and responsible decisions. I will approach municipal issues with honesty, respect and a focus on practical results for the people of Ward 5.
